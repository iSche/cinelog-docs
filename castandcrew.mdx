CineLog's Cast and Crew feature is designed to manage team collaboration securely. Organize your roster using strict industry-standard hierarchies and dynamically control who has access to your sensitive project data.

## Core Features

<CardGrid>
<Card title="Industry Standard Hierarchy">
  Organizes all members into a rigid, 3-level structure (**Group → Department → Role**) reflecting massive studio productions.
</Card>

<Card title="Intelligent Role Planning">
  Start delegating unassigned **Vacant Slots** during pre-production without needing to know exactly who you're hiring yet.
</Card>

<Card title="Role-Based Access Control">
  Share your vision securely. Control what each role is allowed to access across your project, ensuring the right people see the right information.
</Card>

<Card title="Dual Perspectives">

</Card>

## Detailed Usage & Workflows

<Aside type="tip">
Managing a growing crew is chaotic. By establishing a source of truth for Roles and Contacts, every other feature in CineLog becomes automated.
</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Planning Your Crew (Vacant Slots)**

You don't need a single contact in your rolodex to begin staffing. Add standard industry roles (e.g. "Key Grip") to create a **Vacant Slot**. Once you hire someone, simply assign an existing or newly-invited contact to the slot. All departmental defaults will follow the contact intelligently.

</li>
<li>

**Dual Views of Contacts**

Working on a phone or a large monitor? Pick your view. The **Cast and Crew View** gives you a Kanban-style board representing department slots (e.g., Camera -> Grip -> Key Grip). The **Contacts View** acts as a flat, simple rolodex of your contacts natively scoped across your entire workspace.

</li>
<li>

**Granular Project Access & Invitations**

Adding someone to your Cast and Crew does not automatically give them full read permissions. Use the **Intent Toggle** to securely route someone an email invitation bridging their CineLog account to your project. When adjusting permissions, CineLog intelligently resolves individual overrides against departmental defaults so you can confidently upgrade an individual's scope.

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

## Integrated Data Ecosystem

<Aside type="tip">
The Contacts you add and Roles you organize act as the central nervous system for scheduling your production days.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Data Routing Benefits">
-   **Call Sheets Automation**: There is no manual typing in Call Sheets. If you map "Jane Doe" into the Director slot in your Cast and Crew list, she automatically populates at the top of every Call Sheet PDF.
-   **Unified Account Scope**: Your Contacts Rolodex spans the entire workspace. Editing a contact's email or phone number applies globally across every project you've cast them in, removing frustrating duplications.
-   **Batch Selection**: Use selection actions to execute rapid, simultaneous bulk edits across your entire roster instead of repeating simple changes over and over.
</Aside>
