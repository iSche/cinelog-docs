The CineLog Storyboard feature is a visual extension of your Linear Shot List. It provides a dedicated workspace for planning, sequencing, and reviewing the visual composition of your production through intuitive layouts. Storyboards are not a separate set of data—they are a visual-first presentation of the exact same shot data. Every panel on the storyboard is tied 1:1 with a shot in your Linear Shot List.

## Core Capabilities

<CardGrid>
<Card title="Multiple Perspective Layouts">
  View your storyboard horizontally (Linear View), grouped by scene (Scene/Grid View), or full-screen on mobile devices.
</Card>

<Card title="Rich Media Panels">
  Support for multiple image uploads per shot panel. The first image acts as the cover, with the alternatives a swipe away.
</Card>

<Card title="Drag-and-Drop Sequencing">
  Reorder shots visually. Changes immediately sync back to your Shot List.
</Card>

<Card title="Customizable Presentation">

## Detailed Usage & Workflows

### Uploading & Managing Content

Tap to Upload

Tap the image area of a panel to upload media. Your device's image picker opens — photo gallery on phone, file browser on desktop — and the images upload directly to that shot.

Scrub through Sequences

If you upload multiple images to a single panel, tap near the panel's edges to cycle through the sequence, or tap the center to open the expanded view.

Cover Image

The first image in a panel's order is the cover shown in Grid view and included in PDF exports. Reorder a panel's images to change which one is the cover.

### Working with Views

#### Linear View (Desktop/Tablet)

A horizontal timeline. You can adjust the zoom to see ~2 large panels or ~4 smaller panels on screen. Linear is for reviewing the sequence — drag reordering is done in Grid view, not here.

#### Scene / Grid View

Opens a vertical grid (up to 10 columns) of your whole project's shots, grouped by scene with markers between scenes. This provides an excellent high-level overview of your coverage, and it's where reordering happens — long-press a card and drag it, dropping it into another scene's section to move it there. The reorder applies to the underlying shots, so shot letters update to match.

## Pro-Tips & Platform Specifics

### Mobile Phone Linear Carousel

On phones, the Linear view transforms into a full-page swipeable carousel. Swiping directly on the **image area** cycles through multiple images uploaded to that specific shot. Swiping on the **metadata area** at the bottom navigates to the next or previous shot entirely.

### Double-Tap to Edit (Mobile)

On phones, all tappable fields require a **double-tap** to edit to prevent accidental changes while swiping. Tablets and Desktops rely on a single tap.

### PreVis Aspect Ratios

Storyboard views automatically render your images in their true user-selected aspect ratio, including tall vertical formats like 9:16 (TikTok/Reels format) without cropping.

### Professional PDF Export

Generate clean, production-ready PDF sheets directly from your storyboard views. Hit `Cmd+P` to configure a PDF export, with controls for aspect ratio, image fit, columns per page, page size, scene range, and spacing. Every panel renders its image together with its shot metadata.
