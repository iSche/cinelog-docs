## Core Features

<CardGrid>
<Card title="Live Schedule Engine">
  Generate time-accurate schedules automatically based on your estimated scene times. Entering a "Crew Call" cascades out times for your entire day, intelligently warning you of bottlenecks.
</Card>

<Card title="Global Multi-Language PDFs">
  Pixel-perfect export of Tier-1 industry call sheets featuring automatic font fallbacks for **Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Hebrew, Hindi, Ukrainian**, and more.
</Card>

<Card title="Automated Logistics">
  Capture shooting locations with Google Places autocomplete, and inject an accurate weather forecast for your shoot day automatically.

## Detailed Usage & Workflows

<Aside type="tip">
Call Sheets automatically pull data from your Shot List and Cast & Crew rosters, meaning you'll spend less time manually configuring the document, and more time getting ready for framing your shots.
</Aside>

<Steps>
<ol>

<li>

**Generating a Daily Schedule**

Instead of typing every single shot time manually, CineLog's schedule engine does the math for you. Add a crew call, and the engine immediately cascades this time downwards, automatically plotting the exact start times for all subsequent scenes and breaks based on their estimated times.

</li>
<li>

**Managing Talent & Crew Times**

Click into the Talent or Crew sections to prune the list down to exactly who is required that day. Set individualized Call Times, Hair & Make-Up, and Wardrobe times per individual, then drag-and-drop to reorder them by expected arrival.

</li>
<li>

**PDF Export and Email Sendouts**

Ensure everyone knows exactly what's going on. Click **Export** to format an industry-standard PDF bearing your company's logo, or hit **Send** to blast tracked email sendouts right into your team's pocket.

</li>

</ol>
</Steps>

## Integrated Data Ecosystem

<Aside type="tip">
The Call Sheets schedule acts as a bidirectional viewport of your Shot List. Editing a scene order or estimate in the Call Sheet _instantaneously_ updates your underlying Shot List layout via CineSync.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Data Routing Benefits">
-   **Cast and Crew Flows**: Talent names, crew roles, and contact details flow strictly from your central Cast and Crew hub. Assigning an actor dynamically populates their data.
-   **Error Handling Guarantees**: Any invalid schedule bottleneck displays stark yellow/red UI alerts preventing timeline clashes.
-   **Global Access Routing**: Anyone receiving a Call Sheet link is intelligently funneled to create an account, gaining access strictly to what their role dictates.
</Aside>
